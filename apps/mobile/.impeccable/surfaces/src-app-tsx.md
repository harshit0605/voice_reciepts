# Counterwell mobile app (staff and owner)

Scope: every screen of the Expo app (src/). Visitor mode: **Operate**. Light theme only: the use scene is a shop counter under bright tube lights; owners read it at home too, but the counter decides.

Audience and job: counter staff bill fast with a queue waiting; the owner approves, checks money and stock. Constraints: Hindi and English equal; Android and iPhone native guarantees; all functions and copy meaning preserved.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The shop's register, made live. Every rupee is a ruled entry with a name and a time beside it; every decision lands as a dated stamp. Refuses the card-stack SaaS dashboard with a floating + button.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white register paper, pale-blue ruling as the only dividers, a 1px red margin rule on registers, black ink text, ballpoint blue as the single action colour, stamps in their own inks (violet approved, red declined/short, green paid, amber waiting). Anek Devanagari throughout with tabular figures; amounts locked to one right edge.

STORY: Staff see what to do next and do it in one reach; the owner sees who did what, when, and for how much, and trusts it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sell: shop name and sync state, then a full-width search with scan and voice; results as ruled rows (name, strength, stock left; price on the right edge, Add); the current sale as a heavy ink bar pinned above the tabs with item count and a large total.

FORM: Register & Stamp, candidate 4 of 7 on my ordered list; seed key 98d2c81e. Raises: destructive actions isolated by space; one fixed time column; ruling as the real grid; record numbers as identifiers; staff initials marks; hierarchy by size, no boxes around numbers.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
